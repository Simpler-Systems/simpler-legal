§1How the numbers are made

The discipline is walk-forward: every fix must prove itself on documents it has never seen. Each round draws fresh public paper by a fixed rule (fresh search queries, fresh court years, zero overlap with every prior draw), written into the round's draw script. That script was not committed ahead of the draw: for rounds 6 and 7 the rule is in the round write-up committed about a minute before the draw commit (4ecee03 before 81fe4c8, c72b7d7 before a2df071); for round 4, round 5 and the pure-OOS probe it first appears with the draw. None of this is pre-registration. A blind swarm of frontier-model labelers builds gold before the engine touches anything (sealed by commit), the engine runs once, and the board publishes as drawn. One variable per experimental arm. The identity gate — zero DIRECT leaks — is inviolable: an arm that leaks dies, whatever else it improves.

The instrument is measured like the engine: the scorer carries a self-test (8/8), and occurrence counting is word-bounded (v1 counted substrings — Bo "leaked" inside Board; we found it, fixed it, and rescored every baseline). The gold was also re-judged: 240 calls were sampled from the gold of the 100-document development set — 120 courts, 120 business, every one a quasi-identifier or keep-readable call; no identity (DIRECT) label was sampled — and 8 agents re-judged them blind, one mask-or-keep verdict each. They agreed with the gold on 213: 88.8% (courts 89.2, business 88.3). It is not a full relabel and not a ceiling on quasi-identifier recall: 203 of the 240 calls are “keep”, so answering “keep” every time would agree on 84.6%. It was measured on 2026-07-23 (commit a266114), before the doctrine revisions (P0f onward) that round 4's rescored board and the rounds 5–7 boards are scored under, and it was not re-measured under them. node pii-bench/gold-ceiling/score-ceiling.mjs recomputes it from the committed sample, key and verdicts.

§3The post-AI metric: does the analysis survive?

Pre-AI redaction tools measure spans. The real question in 2026 is different: after masking, can a frontier model still do the work? We measured it: 25 original/masked pairs, 5 analytical questions each (obligations, procedure, remedies, risk). The question writer was told not to ask anything whose answer is a name, date, address or other identity, so this measures the substance that survives masking, not what masking removes. One analyst reads only the original, another only the masked copy, and a judge scores substance-equivalence. The judge is not blind: its prompt says which answer came from the redacted copy and that placeholder-for-name substitutions count as equivalent. All three roles are Opus agents (pii-bench/utility-bench/harness-v1.js).

Aggregate utility 0.968 — 121 of 125 answers equivalent, zero partial, 24 of 25 documents at 1.00. The four divergents are all in one document. The result file keeps the judge's reasons for three of them, and each faults the original-side answer, not the masked one: two say it was empty, one that it missed the question. They are counted against us and published as drawn. The 25 documents are round-4 paper, masked by the engine as it stood then, before the freeze.

§4Transfer and the hard cases

  • UK transfer probe: the courts configuration as it stood before the round-6 and round-7 fixes, not changed for the UK, on 10 UK judgments it was never tuned for — 100.0 DIRECT, gate 100%. The frozen configuration has not been run on this set.
  • Court-anonymized family judgments (SGHCF): the engine must preserve the court's own pseudonyms while catching real names the court missed. The parties' codes survive correctly — and the cited-case codes (TYS v TYT [2017]) exposed an instrument find: both our engine and the gold labelers of rounds 5 and 6 missed them. Fixed both sides; round 7 scored the class for the first time: 14/14 preserved. That figure is recorded in ROUND7.md; because the Family Court gold is withheld, it cannot be rescored from the repository.
  • The pure-OOS probe: one document, drawn and run after the engine was frozen, by a fixed rule committed together with its result — an executive transition agreement filed 2026-01-21. It was filed months before the freeze; what makes it unseen is that it was in no earlier draw. Blind gold sealed first; stripped through the live engine adapter. Board: 100.0 / gate 100% — 3 DIRECT spans in its gold, none readable — and the best preservation of the campaign (NM 78.1), on one document.
  • A law firm's own paper: in September the frozen configuration ran on 21 public court filings in the shapes a firm writes — briefs, complaints, settlements, a demand letter, engagement letters, declarations, letter motions, expert papers — of which 20 could be scored. There is no sealed gold here: one labeler listed 296 identifiers by hand. No person on that list (individual parties and counsel included), no email and no phone was left readable. In 14 of the 20, at least one identifier was — 27 of the 296: party companies' names among them (in a caption, a Re: line, a settlement), a law firm's name, other organisation and business names, docket and case numbers, an address fragment, money amounts. Judges and other public officials were not counted, and on papers routed as court judgments the engine releases by design a name it reads as a judge's, or as counsel's on a counsel line, and the name of a court or public body acting officially. In the first draw they sat at an organisation's first mention, in a caption, a Re: line or a defined-term parenthetical, or inside a rail's glue. These numbers rest on the write-up (OPS_LEDGER.md, FREEZE.md), not on a board; the filings are not in the repository.

§5The misses, by class

The brand is honest misses. Every identity that survived a fresh-paper round of this campaign (rounds 4–7 and the pure-OOS probe) is listed here with its diagnosis. Each is either addressed — by a fix, by a doctrine ruling, or, for round 7's two release-side breaks, by a fix re-run on the same documents because no round 8 was drawn — or named on the open shelf. Where the round write-ups record whether a class recurred on later paper, the entry says so; not recurring is weaker than a test, because later paper may hold no instance of the class. What this page does not print is the identifier itself: most of these came from Singapore judgments we are not free to redistribute, and several of them a court had already anonymised. The diagnosis is what carries the engineering; the string would only carry the person. Each is shown as its class, in its real shape. The round write-ups in the repository (ROUND4–7.md) do not yet hold to the same rule and still name some of these people.

Round 4: [given name, inside a court-anonymised judgment] — a real name the court's own anonymisation missed, and the engine too; the most important miss of the round. [initials alias] — an alias the initials rail did not derive. [three surnames, one separation agreement] — several individuals in one business agreement, under-tabled by the substrate; this is the round-4 business leak, 3 spans in 1 of 15 documents. All three classes were the round-5 fix list (ROUND4.md).
Round 5: [surname, shared with a cited judge] — a judge correctly identified and released shared a surname with the masked party, so the release surfaced it; collision veto added, fail-closed. [given name in quotes, after a name run] — a bare quoted nickname with no parenthesis or keyword; nickname rail added. Neither class recurred on round 6. On round 7 the nickname class did not recur; the collision class did once, where a numbered multi-party caption left the veto nothing to check against (round 7's first entry below).
Round 6: [four × 4-digit account tail] — introduced as "account number ending in NNNN", then reused as a defined alias; fragment rail added, all four masked on the same documents in round 7's in-sample race, and no account-fragment leak on round 7's fresh paper (ROUND7.md). [surname of a long-dead composer] — a cultural reference in a judge's flourish, a contested gold label, resolved by doctrine ruling: creators of cited works are not private individuals.
Round 7: [surname] and [three-part personal name + firm] — two release-side breaks, where the engine unmasked correct masks via a caption format and a glue pattern it had never seen; both fixed and re-run on the same documents the same session. [three × SG vehicle plate, format SXX 0000X] — open shelf, rail-able. [initials + numeric suffix] — an affidavit alias, a variant of the round-4 initials-alias class that the round-5 fix does not reach; open shelf. [single given name, non-English] — in a footnote, the hardest of the set. [bare definite role reference] — a contested label, marked DIRECT against the labelling scheme's own role-word rule.

The frozen engine's known limits are stated in FREEZE.md: courts QUASI ran 39–44 on rounds 5–7. Pushing that axis belongs to a compliance profile and was deferred at the freeze — the export-to-AI profile's bar is utility, measured at 0.968. The 88.8% gold agreement in §1 is not a ceiling for it. And stripping withholds identities — it does not claim inference-proofing.

§6Reproduce our numbers

# the scorer proves itself first
node score-pii.mjs selftest                # 8/8 verdicts verified

# re-draw from public sources: this re-runs the search and REWRITES the manifest;
# the EDGAR legs query SEC full-text search live, so a later draw may differ
node build-round7.mjs && node build-pureoos.mjs
git checkout pii-bench/                    # put the pinned manifests back
node backfill-manifest-sha.mjs --check     # MISS for any set you did not rebuild,
                                           # DRIFT if yours differ (exit 3)

# the gold ships as character offsets; rebuild its span text from your corpus
node gold-offsets.mjs hydrate

# rescore a board from committed gold + your own strip. Round 7, the UK probe and the
# pure-OOS probe ship the gold their boards used (less the withheld family-court files,
# WITHHELD.md); rounds 4-6 ship it as re-projected after contact, so of their columns
# only business DIRECT will match (BENCHMARK.md, "Which gold ships")
node score-pii.mjs --stripped <dir> --batch all --name recheck \
  --labels pii-bench/round7-gold-product --docs raw/round7 \
  --manifest pii-bench/round7-manifest.json

# the frozen engine behind one endpoint (needs the local model)
node serve-legal.mjs --port 1436 --llama 49400

Model: Gemma E2B, quantized, not fine-tuned by us, sha256-pinned (3646b4c1…, 3.35 GB), temperature 0, local llama.cpp server. No cloud, no fine-tune, no telemetry. Gold labels (as character offsets, not text), boards, manifests, doctrine projections, and the round narratives (ROUND4–7.md, FREEZE.md, BENCHMARK.md) are committed in the repository. The Singapore Family Court gold is withheld from the current tree, so the courts boards do not fully reproduce; the scorer says so and scores the rest (WITHHELD.md). The repository's history is not yet in that state: commits before the gold became offsets, both of the repository's tags among them, still hold it as span text (the Family Court annotations only in the later tag, v1-legal-frozen; the earlier v1-legal-FROZEN-v12 predates them). Text from Family Court judgments also stands in that history outside the gold: in four commit messages, in leaked spans written out in full in seven run boards (boards have recorded only a category, a length and a digest since 2026-07-26), in ROUND7.md as that tag holds it, and in earlier versions of this page. None of it is to be published until the history is rewritten (README.md, "Data statement"; FREEZE.md, C12 and C13). The stripped outputs the boards scored are not committed, so re-deriving a board means re-running the engine as it stood for that board; the draw scripts re-run the acquisition rather than replay a fixed list, so a later EDGAR rebuild that draws different documents cannot reproduce those boards.
